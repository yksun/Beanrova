Brew guide for pour-over and espresso

Beanrova guides each pour of a pour-over with an animated brewer seen from the side and from above (where to pour, how wide to circle and how fast), scales any recipe to your dose, and records espresso shots with weight, flow and pressure curves. It reads Bluetooth coffee scales and pressure sensors, keeps a bean library and a recipe library with sources, and keeps core brewing available offline. AI coaching is optional. Version 1.1 adds more brewing methods, editable notes, brew photos, optional iCloud sync and Community sharing. Interface languages include English, Simplified/Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French and German.

Questions

Do I need a scale? No. Guided brewing works with the timer alone. To try the live weight features first, open Devices › Connect demo scale; demo readings are labelled as such.

Which devices work? Built-in protocols: BOOKOO scales and Espresso Monitor, 享咖小记 (Coffee Note) protocol scales, Acaia (official SDK), Decent, Felicita, Timemore, Hiroia Jimmy, Eureka Precisa, Varia AKU, Skale, DiFluid Microbalance, Pressensor PRS, Popsicle, Smart Espresso Profiler and Transducer Direct pressure sensors. Devices marked “Beta” in the app follow the maker's published protocol and are still being refined. Reports are welcome.

How does AI work? On-device AI uses Apple Intelligence where available. Cloud AI uses your own API key with the provider you choose and asks before any data is sent. See the privacy policy.

Where is my data? Records are saved on your device. In version 1.1, optional iCloud sync mirrors personal records privately; Community content you choose to publish is public. Settings › Data creates JSON backups or CSV exports. Removing the app removes local data, but existing iCloud, exported and publicly shared copies may remain. See the privacy policy for deletion options.

Are the famous recipes official? They are summarised from the public sources shown in each recipe, for reference. The people and brands named are credited as authors and are not affiliated with Beanrova.

手冲与意式冲煮助手

Beanrova 用侧视和俯视两种动画引导每一段手冲注水（往哪里注、圈多大、绕多快），按你的粉量自动换算任意配方，并用重量、流速和压力曲线记录意式萃取。支持蓝牙咖啡秤和压力计，提供咖啡豆库和带出处的配方库，核心冲煮可离线使用，AI 指导为可选功能。1.1 版加入更多冲煮方式、笔记修改追加、冲煮照片、可选 iCloud 同步和社区分享。界面支持英语、简繁中文、日语、韩语、西班牙语、法语和德语。

常见问题

必须有电子秤吗？ 不需要。只用计时也能完成引导冲煮。想先体验实时重量功能，可在 设备 › 连接演示电子秤；演示数据会明确标注。

支持哪些设备？ 已内置协议：BOOKOO 电子秤与 Espresso Monitor、享咖小记协议电子秤、Acaia（官方 SDK）、Decent、Felicita、Timemore、Hiroia Jimmy、Eureka Precisa、Varia AKU、Skale、DiFluid Microbalance，以及 Pressensor PRS、Popsicle、Smart Espresso Profiler、Transducer Direct 压力计。App 中标为“测试版”的设备按厂家公开的协议支持，仍在完善中，欢迎反馈。

AI 如何工作？ 本机 AI 在支持的设备上使用 Apple 智能。云端 AI 使用你自己的 API Key 和你选择的服务商，发送任何数据前都会先征得同意。详见隐私政策。

数据保存在哪里？ 记录保存在你的设备上。1.1 版可选择使用 iCloud 私有同步，主动发布到社区的内容则是公开的。设置 › 数据 可创建 JSON 备份或导出 CSV。删除 App 会移除本地数据，但已有的 iCloud、导出和公开分享副本可能保留，删除方式见隐私政策。

知名配方是官方的吗？ 配方根据每个配方中注明的公开出处整理，仅供参考。列出的人名和品牌仅表示配方作者，与 Beanrova 没有关联。
