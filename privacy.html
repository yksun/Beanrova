Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · Developer: Yukun Sun · Contact: sunyukun9@gmail.com

Beanrova is a coffee brewing app for iPhone and iPad. It has no account, no server of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing from the app is sent to the developer.

What stays on your device

Your beans, recipes, brews, taste notes and the raw readings from your scale and pressure sensor are stored only on your device. You can create a backup or a CSV export; it is saved or shared only where you choose. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only to connect to coffee scales and pressure sensors you choose, to read weight, flow and pressure and to send commands such as tare. Readings stay on your device.

Camera and photos

The camera or a photo you pick is used only when you scan a coffee bag label or a recipe screenshot. Text is read on the device. A photo leaves the device only if you send it to a cloud AI provider yourself (see below).

AI

AI features are optional; brewing, timers and Bluetooth work without them.

  • On-device AI (Apple Intelligence, where available) runs on your device.
  • Cloud AI is off until you add your own API key for a provider you choose (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, Alibaba Qwen or another compatible service), turn on cloud transfer, and accept a notice that names the provider and lists the data to be sent. Requests go directly from your device to that provider, under its own terms and privacy policy; Beanrova keeps no copy on any server.
  • Depending on the feature, the data sent can include brew measurements, the recipe, bean information, taste notes, a short summary of recent brews, text you paste, or a photo you choose (photos only if you allow image input). Your full history is never sent by default.
  • You can revoke a provider's permission in AI Settings at any time.

API keys

API keys are stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device. They are never included in backups, exports or logs.

Web and local network

Beanrova opens a web page only when you paste its address to import a recipe. It connects to your local network only if you enter the address of your own AI server.

Children

Beanrova is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日 · 开发者：孙宇坤（Yukun Sun）· 联系：sunyukun9@gmail.com

Beanrova 是一款适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 的咖啡冲煮 App。它没有账号，没有自己的服务器，没有广告、分析统计或跟踪。App 不会向开发者发送任何内容。

保存在你设备上的内容

你的咖啡豆、配方、冲煮记录、口味笔记以及电子秤和压力计的原始数据只保存在你的设备上。你可以创建备份或导出 CSV，文件只会保存或分享到你选择的位置。删除 App 即删除这些数据。

蓝牙

蓝牙只用于连接你选择的咖啡秤和压力计，读取重量、流速和压力，并发送去皮等指令。数据保存在你的设备上。

相机和照片

只有在你识别咖啡包装标签或配方截图时，才会使用相机或你选择的照片。文字识别在设备上完成。只有你主动发送给云端 AI 服务商时（见下文），照片才会离开设备。

AI

AI 功能是可选的；冲煮、计时和蓝牙不依赖 AI。

  • 本机 AI（在支持的设备上使用 Apple 智能）在你的设备上运行。
  • 云端 AI 默认关闭。只有当你为自己选择的服务商（例如 OpenAI、Anthropic、Google、DeepSeek、阿里通义千问或其他兼容服务）添加自己的 API Key、开启云端传输，并确认列出服务商和数据类别的提示后才会启用。请求从你的设备直接发送到该服务商，并受其条款和隐私政策约束；Beanrova 不会在任何服务器上保留副本。
  • 根据所用功能，发送的数据可能包括冲煮数据、配方、咖啡豆信息、口味笔记、近期冲煮摘要、你粘贴的文字，或你选择的照片（仅在你允许发送图片时）。默认不会发送完整历史记录。
  • 你可以随时在 AI 设置中撤销对某个服务商的授权。

API Key

API Key 只保存在你设备的 iOS 钥匙串中，绝不会出现在备份、导出文件或日志中。

网页和局域网

只有在你粘贴网址导入配方时，Beanrova 才会打开该网页。只有在你填写自己的 AI 服务器地址时，才会连接局域网。

儿童

Beanrova 并非面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的信息。

变更

如本政策有变更，新版本将发布在本页面并更新生效日期。
